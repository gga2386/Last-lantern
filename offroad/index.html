How to drive

Gas is on the right. Brake at a stop puts you in reverse. Steer with the arrows, or A and D on a keyboard. Space is the handbrake.

LOCK ties the diffs for mud and rock. Hold WINCH to drag yourself forward and back onto your wheels. RESET plants you upright where you are.

Dirt, trail, sand, mud, rock, and creek water all grip differently. The creek is shallow enough to ford and slow enough to respect.

Fuel burns while the engine runs, and it is free — but only a general store pump will fill you. The stores also fit a bigger tank and sell jerry cans you can pour from the CAN button anywhere. Both take reputation, not cash.

Adventures pay reputation. Ranks open tires, suspension, the engine, the winch, the camp kit, and the Ridge Scout at the yard garage. Headlights come on at dusk (AUTO). H cycles them, B is high beam. C cycles the camera. J is the board, G is the garage, E is the store.

Stop on flat ground, or at a marked camp, and tap CAMP. The fire will carry you to morning or evening. Fish the creek, photograph the wildlife (a bow, later, brings meat), and cook at the fire. A meal can steady the tires, stretch the fuel, or sweeten the next adventure.
