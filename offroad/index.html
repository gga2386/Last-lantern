How to drive

The wheel on the left steers and springs back to center. DRIVE is the throttle. STOP brakes, and at a stop it backs you up. D, P, and R are drive, park, and reverse. Park is the parking brake. A keyboard still uses W, A, S, D, and Space, and a gamepad uses the left stick and the triggers. The round button starts and stops the engine.

These are crawlers, not racers. LOW (or K) shortens the gear for torque and a slower crawl. LOCK ties the diffs for mud and rock. Hold WINCH to drag yourself toward a tree or a big rock. Deep mud bogs the chassis. Deep water, past the wading line, stalls the engine. A snorkel and a lift raise that line. RESET only helps when you are upright on firm ground. On your side, roof, or stuck, call for recovery: a yard truck takes about 20 seconds, hooks a line, and pulls you out. That call counts as a reset on an adventure.

Dirt, trail, sand, mud, rock, and creek water all grip differently. The creek is shallow enough to ford and slow enough to respect.

Fuel burns while the engine runs, and it is free — but only a general store pump will fill you. The stores also fit a bigger tank and sell jerry cans you can pour from the CAN button anywhere. Both take reputation, not cash.

Adventures pay reputation. Ranks open tires, suspension, the engine, the winch, the camp kit, and the rest of the yard: the Ridge Scout, Bluff Runner, Creek Wagon, Basin Hauler, Spine Crawler, and Lantern Van. Fitted snorkels, winches, tires, lifts, tanks, and the cans you are carrying show on the rig. The garage can hide any of those parts. Hiding one does not take the part off. Paint in the garage is free. Credits lists every model and texture. Headlights come on at dusk (AUTO). H cycles them, B is high beam.

The basin is three countries. Hollow Forest is home. Red Wash opens at 40 reputation, Granite Spine at 120. Each has its own store, camps, and adventures. Stop on flat ground, or at a marked camp, and tap CAMP. The tent follows the kit, up to a rooftop shell and a tarp. Fish only where there is water. Spot wildlife on the trail with the field kit. Cook only when the pantry has a meal. Roast a marshmallow over a lit fire, once each camp night. Tent and chair colors in the camp kit are free. Chocolate and crackers from the store turn a good one into a s’more. Grey buttons stay on the bar and tell you what is missing.

The garage lists approach, departure, and breakover. The inclinometer on the dash goes gold, then red, as the truck tips. Low graphics turns off the color grade, the vignette, and the drifting dust.
